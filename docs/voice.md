# λledger copy rules

**Style:** Field-manual clarity. Human voice. Minimal words. Not a drill instructor.

## Status → action → confirmation

- **Status:** Say what changed. "Past due."
- **Action:** Give the next decision. "Pay or float."
- **Confirmation:** Say what happened. "Marked sent. Waiting to clear."

Use only the steps needed. Prefer **Due**, **Sent**, **Cleared**, **Floated**, **Short**, and **Left** over technical bookkeeping language.

## Guardrails

- Payday means *scheduled*, never proof a deposit arrived. Tell the user to check the bank.
- Sent means payment initiated, **not** cleared. Never describe sent as paid.
- Floated means payment planning moved. The bill's actual due date stays unchanged.
- A projected remainder is not guaranteed spending money. Identify estimates.
- Shortfalls and past-due bills are facts, not moral failures. Offer a practical next step.
- Lock-screen notifications never contain bill names, dollar amounts, or account details.
- Don't repeat instructions for handled items. If nothing needs action, say so and stop.
- Keep standard controls short: Save, Cancel, Edit, Delete. Explain irreversible deletion.

## Examples

| Context | Preferred copy |
| --- | --- |
| Payday | Payday. Check the deposit. Review the plan. |
| Due today | Bills due today. Pay or float. |
| Past due | Past due. Pay or float what's outstanding. |
| Floated item returns | Back on deck. A floated bill needs a decision. |
| Balance missing | Enter your bank balance to see what's left. |
| Short | Short $125. Uncheck a bill or float it. |
| Payment sent | Marked sent. Waiting to clear. |
| All clear | Nothing to handle. You're clear. |

**Copy-only scope:** no change to financial calculations, payment transitions, notification timing, persistence, or privacy.
